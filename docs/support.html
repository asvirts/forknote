Questions

How do I get Forknote Pro on another device?

Forknote Pro is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing for iPhone and iPad. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings, and tap Restore Purchases.

What does the sentence mean?

It describes a food that shows up alongside a symptom in your own log, more often on symptom days than on quiet days. It is not a conclusion about what you should eat. Forknote is a personal log, not a medical device.

Why didn’t a food I eat every day show up?

A food eaten on most days stays quiet, even when it was also there on symptom days. A food also stays hidden until it appears in at least five meals, with enough symptom days and quiet days to compare.

Where is my data?

On this iPhone or iPad. There is no account and no sync. Use Settings → Delete All Local Data to erase the log on that device.
