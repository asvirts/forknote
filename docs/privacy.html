Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Forknote for iPhone and iPad

Short version: Forknote does not collect, transmit, rent, or sell your meals, symptoms, or any other personal data. There is no account and no analytics or advertising SDK.

1. Data we collect: none

Andrew Virts (“we”) does not collect the foods you type, the symptoms you log, notes, or usage data from Forknote.

2. Where your log lives

Meals, symptoms, and your symptom names are stored on your iPhone or iPad in Forknote’s private SwiftData store. They are not uploaded to a server we operate. There is no iCloud sync in this version.

The Lock Screen control and the app share a flag on your device through an Apple App Group so the control can open the meal screen. That flag does not leave the device.

3. Purchases

Forknote Pro is processed by Apple through the App Store with StoreKit 2. We never receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether Forknote Pro is unlocked on this Apple Account.

4. The PDF

When you export a PDF, Forknote builds the file on your device. It is shared only with the destination you pick in the system share sheet.

5. Deleting your log

  • Delete a meal or symptom from the log.
  • Use Settings → Delete All Local Data to remove meals, symptoms, and custom names from this device.
  • Deleting the app removes the local store from that device.

6. Children

Forknote does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it. Questions: asvirts@outlook.com.
