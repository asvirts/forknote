2. A personal log

Forknote is a personal log. It is not a medical device. The sentence describes what shows up in the meals and symptoms you entered. It does not tell you what is wrong or what to change.

Talk with a clinician about your health. Do not use Forknote as a substitute for that conversation.

Logging stays free. Forknote Pro is an optional one-time, non-consumable purchase through the App Store, with Family Sharing. Billing and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The log lives on your device. We cannot restore it if the device is erased, because there is no account and no server copy. Export a PDF when you want a copy, and keep device backups on if you want Apple’s backup of the app.
